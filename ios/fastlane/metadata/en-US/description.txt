PraticOS is the work order app for service professionals and small service businesses: HVAC technicians, mechanics, electricians, plumbers, phone repair, maintenance and many more. Say what you are going to do and AI fills in the work order for you. From quote to payment, everything stays on your phone.

SAY IT AND THE WORK ORDER FILLS ITSELF
• Tap Speak and say the job: "Julia's Civic, oil change and wheel alignment"
• Or snap a photo of the license plate or a handwritten request
• AI fills in customer, vehicle, services and parts, with the prices from your catalog
• Everything AI filled in is marked for you to check, and you can undo it
• Fix it by voice too: "alignment is 100", "remove the balancing"

WHAT TO DO NEXT
• Home shows your work orders and the next step for each one
• Open a new work order in seconds with the "New order" button
• Clear status: quote, approved, in progress, completed

QUOTES AND WORK ORDERS
• Services, parts and prices on one screen
• Job photos and inspection checklists
• Work order PDF with photos, ready to send or print

CUSTOMERS FOLLOW ALONG BY LINK
• Send the work order over WhatsApp with a secure link
• The customer sees the progress and approves the quote through the link
• Get the customer's rating when the job is done

SCHEDULE
• Your appointments on a calendar
• A reminder before each one

FINANCIAL
• See how much you still have to collect, and from whom
• Record what the customer paid, all at once or in parts

CUSTOMERS
• Service history, vehicles and devices for each customer
• How much each customer still owes

YOUR WAY
• Pick your line of work and the app uses your words: vehicle, device, equipment
• Dark mode and large text
• Sign in with Apple, Google or email

PLANS AND SUBSCRIPTIONS
PraticOS is free to start. The Free plan has monthly limits on photos, checklists and AI use. The Starter, Pro and Business plans raise these limits and are auto-renewable monthly subscriptions purchased in the app.
• Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase.
• The subscription renews automatically unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
• Manage or cancel your subscription at any time in your App Store account settings.
