IN-APP PURCHASE (GUIDELINE 3.1.1) - PLEASE READ

PraticOS is a personal work-order tool for independent service professionals (air-conditioning technicians, mechanics, phone repair, cleaning, etc.). There is no business or organization registration in the app: on first launch the user picks their line of work and, optionally, the name their customers will see on work orders. Everything after that is the user's own account.

All paid features are sold only through In-App Purchase:
- The Free plan has monthly limits on photos, checklist templates and people on the account. Paid plans raise these limits and remove the watermark from PDF work orders.
- Inviting another person to work on the same account ("Equipe") is a paid feature. On the Free plan, tapping "Convidar" opens the In-App Purchase plans.
- Paid plans are auto-renewable monthly subscriptions sold through In-App Purchase: Starter, Pro and Business (subscription group "PraticOS").
- Paid plans cannot be bought anywhere else from the app: the app has no external purchase links or calls to action, and the website shows no prices.

Where to find the paywall:
1. Sign in with the demo account (steps below).
2. On the "Início" (Home) tab, tap the profile button at the top.
3. Tap "Plano" (Plan), then "Ver planos" (View plans).

The paywall shows each plan with its price and period, the auto-renewal terms, Terms of Use, Privacy Policy and a Restore Purchases button.

The payment features in work orders let the professional record and charge their own customers for physical, real-world services performed outside the app (guideline 3.1.3(e)); they are not payments to PraticOS.
