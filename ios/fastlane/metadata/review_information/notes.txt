IN-APP PURCHASE (GUIDELINE 3.1.1) - PLEASE READ

PraticOS is a personal work-order tool for independent service professionals (air-conditioning technicians, mechanics, phone repair, cleaning, etc.). There is no business or organization registration in the app: on first launch the user picks their line of work and, optionally, the name their customers will see on work orders. Everything after that is the user's own account.

All paid features are sold only through In-App Purchase:
- The Free plan includes up to 10 work orders a month, 1 person on the account, the checklists of the chosen line of work and the AI assistant (included in every plan). PDF work orders carry the PraticOS brand.
- Paid plans raise these limits and add features: Starter (up to 50 work orders a month, 2 people, service invoices, PDFs with only the user's brand, own checklists), Pro (up to 150 work orders a month, 5 people, roles, a schedule per person, job stages, TV board, contracts) and Business (20 people and 500 work orders a month included, bulk actions, ChatGPT/Claude connection and API).
- Inviting another person to work on the same account ("Equipe") is a paid feature. On the Free plan, tapping "Convidar" shows the plan limit with a "Ver planos" (View plans) button that opens the In-App Purchase plans.
- Paid plans are auto-renewable subscriptions sold through In-App Purchase, each one monthly or yearly: Starter, Pro and Business (subscription group "PraticOS", product IDs praticos_starter_monthly, praticos_starter_annual, praticos_pro_monthly, praticos_pro_annual, praticos_business_monthly, praticos_business_annual).
- Paid plans cannot be bought anywhere else from the app: the app has no external purchase links or calls to action.

What the demo account shows (new plan limits):

The new plan limits are switched on for the demo account, which is on the Free plan. With it you will see:
1. On the "Início" (Home) tab, tap the profile button at the top, then "Plano" (Plan). The screen shows the current plan (Free) and what is being used this month, for example "OS este mês" (work orders this month) out of 10, and people on the account out of 1.
2. When the account gets close to 10 work orders in a month, the app shows a notice with the count. Going over in one month never blocks anything; if it happens again the next month, only the new work order waits for a plan change. Work orders already open always keep working.
3. Paid features show the plan they belong to and a "Ver planos" (View plans) button that opens the In-App Purchase paywall.

Where to find the paywall:
1. Sign in with the demo account (steps below).
2. On the "Início" (Home) tab, tap the profile button at the top.
3. Tap "Plano" (Plan), then "Ver planos" (View plans).

The paywall shows each plan with its price and period, the auto-renewal terms, Terms of Use, Privacy Policy and a Restore Purchases button.

The payment features in work orders let the professional record and charge their own customers for physical, real-world services performed outside the app (guideline 3.1.3(e)); they are not payments to PraticOS.

Signing in with the demo account:

1. On the sign-in screen, tap "Já tenho conta com e-mail" (I already have an account with email) below the Apple and Google buttons.
2. Enter the email and password from the Sign-In Information section of this review.
3. Tap "Entrar" (Sign in).

The demo account has fictional sample data: 6 customers with their vehicles, 8 work orders in every status (quote, approved, in progress, completed), a completed vehicle check-in checklist with photos, and payments in the "Financeiro" tab. All features can be tested with it.
