All paid plans in PraticOS are sold only through In-App Purchase. PraticOS is a freemium app:

- The Free plan has up to 10 work orders a month, 1 person on the account and 10 AI assistant uses a month. It does not unlock any content sold outside the app.
- The paid plans (Starter, Pro and Business) raise those limits and add features. They are auto-renewable subscriptions in the subscription group "PraticOS", each one monthly or yearly. The yearly subscriptions are attached to this version for review.
- The app has no links or calls to action for purchases outside the app.

To see the paywall, sign in with the demo account, tap the profile button at the top of the "Início" (Home) tab, then "Plano" (Plan) and "Ver planos" (View plans). The App Review notes have the full steps.
